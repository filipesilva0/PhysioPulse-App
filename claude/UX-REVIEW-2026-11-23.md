# UI/UX review for the big overhaul (23 Nov 2026)

Owner: dislikes the current design and overall UI/UX, wants a big overhaul; asked first for a prioritized list.

**How this was done.** Read `DESIGN.md`, `STATUS.md`, the Nov 10 audit and the page code; built `build:preview` and clicked through sign-in, Today, Agenda, Patients, a patient file (Anamnese, Evolução), Finance, Team, Settings, the assistant and the New Appointment sheet, as owner, in light and dark, at ~1280 px and against the 1440 px screenshots in `references/`. Counted styles in `src/styles/app.css`.
**Not covered:** the real Windows/Mac build, therapist and reception views, the phone app.

## What's worth keeping
- Teal as the single accent, flat canvas, white cards: calm and clinical. Keep the palette.
- Safety patterns (hold-to-delete, Undo notes, sheets that don't close on outside click, AI that previews before applying).
- Patient list rows ("Próxima terça, 24/11, 10:00") and Finance › Resumo are close to right already.

## Priority 1 — structure (this is most of "I don't like it")

1. **The title bar is three toolbars fighting.** Leading: profile, Settings, assistant, Back, Forward. Centre: 5 page pills. Trailing: 5 text menus + caption buttons. Below ~1340 px the pills lose their names to make room for the menus, so on a normal laptop the main navigation is five unlabelled icons. Fix: on Windows fold Ficheiro/Editar/Ver/Ir/Ajuda into one menu button (≡) at the leading edge, keep shortcuts; pills always show names; Settings moves to the profile menu (it is not a page people visit daily); assistant stays as one button on the trailing side.
2. **The patient file opens on a wall of form fields.** It lands on Anamnese, which is always-editable textareas; red flags and "Guardar" are at the bottom; the tabs scroll away; Evolução puts a full new-note builder above the history. Fix: a **Resumo** tab first (alerts and red flags, missing consents, active prescription with sessions left, last 2 notes, next appointment, balance), read mode by default with Editar per section, sticky tabs, Evolução shows the history first and "Nova nota" opens the builder.
3. **Text is too small and there is no type scale.** 17 different font sizes, the most used is 12 px; body is 13 px, which is a Mac size, too small with Segoe UI on Windows (where the owner works). `--text2` (#545B59) and `--text3` (#5F6764) are effectively the same grey. Fix: 5 tokens (title 26, section 17, body 14, secondary 13, caption 12), one secondary grey, tabular figures kept. Biggest single "looks better" change.
4. **Today wastes the bento.** "Agenda de hoje" is a tall card with 4 rows and half a screen empty; "O seu dia" is 48 thin bars that need explaining; every row carries a "Pacote" tag; "A tratar" mixes 5 icon colours and truncates its lines. Fix: lead with **"A seguir"** (next patient, time, room/therapist, Abrir ficha / Começar nota), then the day as a compact timeline with a now-line, "A tratar" grouped (3 pedidos, 10 lembretes, 2 consentimentos) with counts instead of sentences; tags only when they change what you do (e.g. "Falta pagar", "1.ª consulta").
5. **Agenda spends three rows on date navigation before the grid.** Arrows + Hoje + Dia/Semana/Mês + Nova marcação, then therapist chips, then a week strip that repeats the arrows. Fix: one toolbar row (‹ Hoje › · date · Dia/Semana/Mês · therapist filter · Nova marcação); drop the week strip in Day view or make it the only navigator. Show free slots in the grid itself (click-to-book and drag already exist, but nothing hints at them).

## Priority 2 — consistency and polish

6. **Too many ways to look like a button.** Radii in use: 2, 6, 7, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 24, 999 px; 460 inline `style={{}}` in the pages. Fix: radius tokens (8 controls, 12 fields, 20 cards, pill), move inline styles into components; build a small component kit (Button, Field, Row, Card, Tag, Toolbar) and use it everywhere.
7. ~~Ellipsis on everything.~~ *Withdrawn on checking the HIG:* buttons that open a sheet asking for more input ("Nova marcação…", "Registar pagamento…", "Retirar…" with its confirmation) correctly end in "…". Keep them.
8. **Settings shows the same list twice.** Left column and the "Visão geral" page list the same sections (and in different orders). Fix: drop Visão geral, open on Conta; group the left column (Conta · Clínica · Sistema) with small headers.
9. **The side columns are empty white slabs.** In Agenda, Finanças and Definições a 180–248 px full-height card holds 4–13 items and then nothing. Fix: plain sidebar on the canvas (no card), or move Agenda's 4 items into the toolbar as a segmented control and keep the column only where there are many sections.
10. **Sliders that look set when they aren't.** In the note builder Dor/Rotação/Função thumbs sit in the middle with "—" as the value. Fix: an empty track with "Tocar para avaliar" until touched, value shown large beside it.
11. **Status colour is decoration in places.** A tratar icons in amber/teal/red, a red "Resultado" figure, "• IA" tag on a team member. Fix: colour only for state that needs action (overdue, failed, recording); explain or remove the "IA" tag ("Usa o assistente").
12. **Assistant panel.** Opens with a 9-line paragraph listing everything it can do and the model id ("gemma4:12b") in the header. Fix: one line + the 4 suggestions; model name in Settings only.

## Priority 3 — quick fixes found while testing

13. **2-step code boxes drop digits when typed fast** (typing "123456" in one go kept only "1"). Accept paste and fast typing into the first box and spread it.
14. Team list shows "4/11, 10:02" with no label; say "Última entrada 4/11, 10:02".
15. Unlabelled `↗` buttons on Today cards; use "Ver agenda" / "Ver tudo" text, which already exists at the bottom.
16. "Nota de evolução" in the patient header does nothing visible when you are already on Evolução; scroll to and focus the new note.
17. Sign-in: the logo line takes the top 40% of the window; shrink it so the form sits in the optical centre.
